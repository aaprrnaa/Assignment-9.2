A NOTE ON CREATIVITY

Learning to see differently

Photography is not only about the places we visit. It is also about the way we choose to see them. A familiar street, a passing cloud, or sunlight falling across a room can become a meaningful story.

Paying attention to small details helps us appreciate the ordinary. It encourages curiosity, patience, and a deeper connection with our surroundings.

Whether you are exploring a distant landscape or walking through your own neighborhood, take a moment to look closer. Inspiration often appears when we least expect it.
